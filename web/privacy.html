Privacy

This site is a few static pages and the skill's Markdown file. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics or trackers, has no forms, and loads nothing from any other server: no fonts, no scripts, no images.

The "Try a line" bench on the home page runs in your browser. What you paste stays in that tab and is never sent anywhere.

The host

The site is hosted on Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel receives each request, including your IP address and browser details, and handles it under its own privacy policy.

The skill

Once installed, the skill runs on your machine, inside your own agent, and sends nothing to its author. Its probe script sends requests only to the site you point it at and, with --anon-read, to that site's Supabase project.
