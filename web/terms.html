Not legal advice

The legal checks are a starting point that tells you what to ask a lawyer. Penalty figures are statutory maximums or reported awards. They set priority and are not predictions.

Not a pentest

For a system that moves money, holds health records, or has a team of engineers, run the audit, then get a professional pentest and a lawyer.

Only what you own

Run the live checks, --anon-read, and the attack pass only against sites and projects you own, staging first, with test accounts and nothing destructive.
